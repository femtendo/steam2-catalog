Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Short version

This site collects no personal information. We run a simple, privacy-respecting visit counter and a cookieless analytics beacon. No accounts, no ads, no trackers, no cookies.

What we collect

  • Anonymous visit counts. A counter increments once per browser per day (a daily-rotating anonymous identifier stored in your browser's local storage). We store the count and nothing about you.
  • Aggregate page counts. Which pages are visited (home, games, a depot id), counted anonymously, so we know what to improve. No IPs are stored with these counts.

What we do NOT collect

  • No names, emails, or account information (the site has no accounts)
  • No cookies, no cross-site tracking, no advertising, no fingerprinting
  • No IP addresses are logged by the site itself. Standard, short-lived web-server logs kept by our static host (GitHub Pages) are governed by GitHub's privacy statement.

Local storage

The visitor counter uses one local-storage key containing a random daily token. It is not used for identification, is never transmitted with your identity, and you can clear it anytime by clearing site data in your browser.

Third parties

The site is static and self-contained. Download links point to third-party community mirrors; if you follow them, those services' own policies apply. GitHub hosts this page.

Contact

Privacy questions or takedown requests: open an issue on the project tracker.
