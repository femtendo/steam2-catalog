Community — help catalog the archive

How a depot gets its name

  1. Machine evidence. Every depot's file manifest is parsed automatically — the folders and paths inside are recorded as-is.
  2. Curated names. Depots already identified by the community carry those names (from the steam2-winfsp label table).
  3. Your suggestions. Anything unnamed or mislabeled shows on this site with its manifest evidence. Click Suggest an ID, fill in the prefilled GitHub issue, submit.
  4. Review. Maintainers check the evidence and confirm or comment. Confirmed IDs land in the label table and the site updates.
  5. Credit. Every accepted ID credits its submitter in the commit and the discovery notes.

About

This is an index of the Steam2 content-server archive — Valve's pre-SteamPipe content delivery system, spanning roughly 2003–2013. The archive holds — files across — depots (~12 TB of .dat payloads plus ~16 GB of .blob metadata).

This site catalogs the metadata only: depot names, version history, and the complete file manifest (paths, sizes, hashes) of every depot — no game files are hosted here. Download links point to the public mirrors run by the community.

Depot names are derived from the annotated TeraRelease index, curated and published in the steam2-winfsp project. Manifests are parsed from the blobs with an independent reader.

Metadata index — no copyrighted payloads are hosted or redistributed.
